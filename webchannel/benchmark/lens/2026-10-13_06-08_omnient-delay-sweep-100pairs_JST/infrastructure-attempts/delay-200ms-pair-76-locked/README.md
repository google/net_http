# Device-lock startup failure

The OFF trial could not begin. Xcode reported that observing system notifications failed, development services needed to be enabled, and the device needed to be unlocked. No Lens measurement was started. Files are retained outside the 100 measurement pairs. The first 75 pairs at 200 ms and all 300 pairs at earlier delays were retained. After the user unlocked the device, pair 76 was resumed and the remaining 25 pairs were recorded. Recorded measurement trials, including the 0 ms timeout, are not repeated.
